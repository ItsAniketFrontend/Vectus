Technology

How the system fits together

The website (Next.js) and the backend (PHP and MySQL) are separate. Your content lives in one place and shows up everywhere it's used.

Website

Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Tailwind CSS

Pages are built on the server, so they load fast and Google can read every word.

Animation

GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, Flip) · Lenis

Smooth scrolling, text reveals and scroll-driven product assembly. Animation is reduced automatically on slow phones and for people who prefer less motion.

Backend and admin

PHP 8.3+ · Laravel · Filament

One admin panel for content, SEO, products, orders, careers and warranty, with separate access for each team.

Database

MySQL 8 · Redis cache

Content, orders and warranty records in one database, with a cache to keep pages fast.

Hosting and security

Cloudflare · AWS Mumbai or your server · SSL

Servers in India, a firewall and bot protection in front, and daily backups.

Connected services

Razorpay · Shiprocket · DLT SMS · WhatsApp · Google

Payments, delivery, OTP login and messages, the dealer map, Analytics and Search Console.

What we need from you

Inputs that keep the 21 days on track

The timeline starts when the Day 1 items reach us. If something arrives late, go-live usually moves by about the same number of days.

By Day 1So we can start
  • Shopify admin access. A staff account so we can export products, collections, pages, blog posts and images.
  • Google Search Console, Analytics (GA4) and Tag Manager access. To protect current rankings and keep tracking working.
  • Domain and DNS access. Needed on launch day. We copy your email and employee-portal records first so nothing else breaks.
  • Hosting decision. Your own AWS or cloud account, or we set it up for you.
  • One decision-maker. A single contact who can approve things within 24 hours.
By Day 2For design and the store
  • Logo files, brand colours, fonts and brand guidelines.
  • Which products are sold online. The list of products (or sizes) with Buy Now. Everything else stays enquiry only.
  • Details for online products. Selling price, MRP, weight, dimensions, HSN code and GST rate.
By Day 3Accounts, warranty and animation
  • Razorpay account with KYC complete, and its API keys.
  • Shiprocket account and your pickup or warehouse address.
  • GSTIN and invoice details. Company name and address to print on GST invoices.
  • SMS provider with a DLT-approved sender ID and OTP template. Plus WhatsApp Business API access, if you have it.
  • QR code details. A photo of a current product label, what the QR opens today, whether each unit should get its own code, and who prints the labels.
  • Warranty terms for each product line. Years, conditions, and whether an invoice is required.
  • CAD/3D files or detailed photos of 1–2 flagship products for the assembly animation, for example the Ten-X tank.
By Day 5People, lists and policies
  • Dealer and distributor list in Excel. Name, address, pincode and phone for the dealer locator.
  • Who receives enquiries for each state. Names and email addresses.
  • HR details. Current job openings and the email address for applications.
  • Service team contacts for warranty claims, by region.
  • Policy text, or approval of our drafts. Refund, shipping, cancellation, warranty and privacy.
  • Staff who need admin logins. Names and roles for SEO, content, HR, sales, store and service.
DuringQuick turnarounds
  • Design feedback within 24 hours of each share (approval on Day 4).
  • New photos or copy by Day 7. Otherwise we move the existing content as it is.
  • Testing feedback as one combined list between Day 16 and Day 19.
